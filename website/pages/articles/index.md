# Articles

Every `*.sgit.ai` site carries its own articles: the person's voice memos written up on that site's own dimension, and the agents writing what they delivered, with the commits and the tests to check it against. They are in the newsroom's format so one can be imported into [newsroom.sgit.ai](https://newsroom.sgit.ai) unchanged; the guidance for the pattern is on [send.sgit.ai](https://send.sgit.ai/articles/every-site-its-own-articles.html).
