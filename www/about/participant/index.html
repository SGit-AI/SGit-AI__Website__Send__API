Participant disclosure

This site is published by the people who build the thing it describes. The sgit project (the sgit CLI, SG/Send, SG/Vault and the *.sgit.ai sites) is led by Dinis Cruz, and this repository is written by agents working from his briefs. The claims on these pages are therefore the claims of a participant, not of a reviewer.

What the site does about that: every feature carries a status from the reality file, generated from the code; every number is counted by a script named beside it; the brief is published in full with its corrections beside it; and the tests, the manifest and the live check are there for anyone to run rather than to take on trust. Where a claim is a position, it says so.
